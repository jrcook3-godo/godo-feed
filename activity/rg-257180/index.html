Chamberlain's Ranch Trailhead

Chamberlain's Ranch Trailhead is a hiking trail in Zion National Park, Utah — something to do any time on Go Do.

Where: Zion National Park, UT · Washington County

Phone: 435-644-1300

Chamberlain’s Ranch Trailhead (also called North Fork Trailhead) provides the primary access to the North Fork Virgin River Wilderness Study Area and is the starting point for top-down hikes into The Narrows in Zion National Park.
