Ouray National Fish Hatchery

Ouray National Fish Hatchery is a nature area & viewpoint in Naples, Utah — something to do any time on Go Do.

Where: Naples, UT · Uintah County

Phone: (435) 630-0027

The Ouray National Fish Hatchery was established in 1996 to provide refuge, propagation, and technical development to assist in the recovery of the four endangered Colorado River fish: razorback sucker, Colorado pikeminnow, bonytail, and humpback chub. Ouray National Fish Hatchery is open to the pub
