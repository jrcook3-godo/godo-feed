The Silver Museum LLC

The Silver Museum LLC is a museum in Millcreek, Utah (Salt Lake County) — something to do any time on Go Do. There are 112 more museums within 30 miles.

Where: Millcreek, UT · Salt Lake County
2850 E 3300 S Ste 103, Millcreek

Open in Go Do — make plans with friendsTheir website

The Silver Museum has a collection of over 400 pieces of Historic and Elegant Silver available to preview virtually and in person. Education about antique and museum quality silver is our primary goal. We also purchase your rare unwanted silver items. We love antique silver sterling flatware.
