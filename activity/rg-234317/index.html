Paradise Guard Station

Paradise Guard Station is a campground in Maeser, Utah — something to do any time on Go Do.

Where: Maeser, UT · Uintah County

Overview Paradise Guard Station is located on the Ashley National Forest, approximately 25 air miles northwest of Vernal, in eastern Utah.
