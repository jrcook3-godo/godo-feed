Tasha Equestrian

Tasha Equestrian is a campground in Koosharem, Utah — something to do any time on Go Do.

Where: Koosharem, UT · Sevier County

Overview Tasha Equestrian Campground is located in central Utah, just 4 miles north of beautiful Fish Lake at an elevation of 9,000 feet. Visitors enjoy horseback riding and fishing for famous Mackinaw lake trout. Recreation Many miles of horseback riding, hiking, biking and off-road vehicle trails
