Davis Conference Center

Davis Conference Center is a community center in Layton, Utah — something to do any time on Go Do.

Where: Layton, UT · Davis County
1651 North 700 West, Layton

Phone: +1 801-416-8888

The Davis Conference Center is a conference center with over 70,000-square-feet of flexible meeting space located in Layton, Utah, United States. The center, which hosts 700 groups a year, is unique in that it offers exhibit space, conference space and an adjoining hotel for lodging. In the conference's first year it hosted 726 events.
