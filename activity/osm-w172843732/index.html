Pioneer Park

Pioneer Park is a park & garden in Salt Lake City, Utah — something to do any time on Go Do.

Where: Salt Lake City, UT · Salt Lake County
350 S 300 W, Salt Lake City

Phone: +18015357800

Pioneer Park is a public park in Salt Lake City's Rio Grande neighborhood, in the U.S. state of Utah. The site is listed on the National Register of Historic Places as Old Pioneer Fort Site. The park is the only major green space in downtown Salt Lake City.
