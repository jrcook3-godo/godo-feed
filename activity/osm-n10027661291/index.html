Rush Funplex

Rush Funplex is a family fun center in Orem, Utah — something to do any time on Go Do.

Where: Orem, UT · Utah County

Hours: Mon–Wed 10 AM–10 PM; Thu 10 AM–6 PM; Fri, Sat 10 AM–12 AM; Sun 12 PM–8 PM

The Rush Funplex at University Place Mall in Orem, Utah is your ultimate indoor destination for year-round family fun and entertainment! Explore exciting attractions including go karts, laser tag, mini golf, bumper cars, bowling, foam pit, rock climbing, trampoline, trapeze swing, billiards, and a huge selection of arcade games.
