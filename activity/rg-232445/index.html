Watchman Campground is a campground in Springdale, Utah — something to do any time on Go Do.

Where: Springdale, UT · Washington County

Overview For visitors to Zion National Park, Watchman Campground is an ideal place to stay. Located near the park's south entrance, the campground is just a short walk from the Zion Canyon Visitor Center, the Zion Canyon Shuttle System and the adjacent town of Springdale.
