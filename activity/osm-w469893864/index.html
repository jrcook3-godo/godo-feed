Magna Community Pool

Magna Community Pool is a public pool in Magna, Utah — something to do any time on Go Do.

Where: Magna, UT · Salt Lake County
3270 South 8400 West, Magna

Hours: Mon–Sat 12 PM–6 PM; Sun 12 PM–4 PM

Phone: 3854681835

The Magna pool offers a zero degree beach entry, 25 yard lap length, two diving boards, two water slides, and a splash pad.
