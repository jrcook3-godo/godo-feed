Dance Hall Rock Historic Site

Where: Big Water, UT · Kane County

Phone: 435-826-5499

Dance Hall Rock is a large sandstone amphitheater located 42 miles down the Hole-in-the-Rock Road. On their way to establish a settlement, Mormon pioneers in 1879-80 spent two months waiting for a road to be built to the Colorado River.
