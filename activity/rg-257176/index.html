Elephant Cove Staging Area

Elephant Cove Staging Area is a nature area & viewpoint in Hildale, Utah (Washington County) — something to do any time on Go Do. There are 126 more nature areas & viewpoints within 30 miles.

Where: Hildale, UT · Washington County

Open in Go Do — make plans with friendsTheir website

Phone: 435-644-1300

The Elephant Cove Staging Area provides a parking and staging location for ATV/UTV enthusiasts interested in exploring the fantastic red-rock scenery and four-wheel-drive roads that make up the western side of the BLM Kanab Field Office.
