The Rush Funplex in Pleasant View, Utah is the ultimate indoor entertainment center for all ages! Enjoy bowling, laser tag, mini golf, bumper cars, go karts, kiddie karts, rock climbing, foam pit activities, billiards, and a large arcade—all under one roof. With everything 100% indoors, The Rush offers year-round fun for families, friends, birthdays, and group events.
