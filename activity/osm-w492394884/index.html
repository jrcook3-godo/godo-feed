Shades Brewing in Salt Lake City offers award-winning craft beer brewed on-site, scratch-made food, and a vibrant brewpub atmosphere. Enjoy live music, weekend brunch, sports watch parties, and private events. Located on the 300W bike path with a dog-friendly patio,near central city and University. Shades is the go-to spot for locals and visitors to connect over great beer, food, and community.
