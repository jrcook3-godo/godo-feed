Utah Olympic Oval

Utah Olympic Oval is a ice skating in Kearns, Utah — something to do any time on Go Do.

Where: Kearns, UT · Salt Lake County
5662 S Cougar Ln, Kearns

Phone: +18019686825

The Utah Olympic Oval is an indoor speed skating oval located 14 miles (23 km) southwest of Salt Lake City, in Kearns, Utah. The Oval was built for the 2002 Winter Olympics and it hosted the long-track speed skating events for the 2002 games, a role it is expected to reprise for the 2034 Winter Olympics.
