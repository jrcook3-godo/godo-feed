Lion House

Lion House is a attraction in Salt Lake City, Utah — something to do any time on Go Do.

Where: Salt Lake City, UT · Salt Lake County
63 South Temple, Salt Lake City

Accessibility: Wheelchair accessible

The Lion House is a large residence built in 1856 by Brigham Young, second president of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (LDS Church), in Salt Lake City, Utah. Used for a variety of purposes following the death of Young, the building was closed in 2020, due to the COVID-19 pandemic and has remained closed for renovation and restoration, with an expected reopening in spring 2027.
