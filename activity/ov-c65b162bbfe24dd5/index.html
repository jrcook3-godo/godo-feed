Hogle Zoo

Hogle Zoo is a zoo & aquarium in West Valley City, Utah (Salt Lake County) — something to do any time on Go Do. It’s open Mon–Sun 9 AM–6 PM. There are 21 more zoos & aquariums within 30 miles.

Utah's Hogle Zoo creates champions for wildlife. Visitors of all ages can enjoy hundreds of resident animals in the African Savanna, Great Apes area, Primate Forest, Small Animal Building, Rocky Shores, Asian Highlands, and the new Aline W. Skaggs Wild Utah exhibit. Follow us on Instagram (@HogleZoo) for daily programs, including keeper chats and animal demonstrations.
