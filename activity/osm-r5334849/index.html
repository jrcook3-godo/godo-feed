Goosenecks State Park

Goosenecks State Park is a nature area & viewpoint in Mexican Hat, Utah — something to do any time on Go Do.

Where: Mexican Hat, UT · San Juan County
UT-316, Mexican Hat

Phone: +14356782238

Goosenecks State Park is a state park in the U.S. state of Utah, overlooking a deep meander of the San Juan River. The park is located near the southern border of the state a short distance from Mexican Hat, Utah. Millions of years ago, the Monument Upwarp forced the river to carve incised meanders over 1,000 feet (300 m) deep as the surrounding landscape slowly rose in elevation.
