Upper Narrows Campground

Upper Narrows Campground is a campground in Grantsville, Utah — something to do any time on Go Do.

Where: Grantsville, UT · Tooele County

Cost: $16–$105

Phone: +1-801-733-2660

There are six single sites; no reservations as singles but you can reserve sites 1-4 and the USFS treats it as a small group site or reserve all 6 to serve as a big group site. 16/50/105 fees are confirmed May 2021. Pets must be on a leash.
