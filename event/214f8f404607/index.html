Frautshi, Manasse, & Nakamatsu Trio

When: Wednesday, March 17, 2027 at 7:30 PM
Where: Libby Gardner Concert Hall, Salt Lake City, UT
Price: $11.54–$74.57

Open in Go Do — see who’s going

Address: 1375 E Presidents Circle, Salt Lake City

Jennifer Frautschi (violin), Jon Manasse (clarinet), and Jon Nakamatsu (piano) continue our 61st season on Wednesday, March 17, 2027, at Libby Gardner Concert Hall on the University of Utah campus. A trio of individually celebrated soloists, they bring together a remarkable constellation of accolades: Nakamatsu is the Gold Medalist of the 1997 Van Cliburn International Piano Competition; Frautschi is a two-time Grammy nominee and Avery Fisher Career Grant recipient who performs on a 1722 Stradivarius violin; and Manasse—called "absolutely first-rate" by the New York Times—is Artistic Director
