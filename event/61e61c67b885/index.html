Yoga: December Rise and Shine Yoga Starts – Wednesdays

When: Wednesday, December 2, 2026 at 5:00 AM

Where: Ernie Miller Nature Center - 909 N. Hwy 7 Olathe KS 66061, Cedar Hills, UT
Ernie Miller Nature Center - 909 N. Hwy 7 Olathe KS 66061

Start your day with an invigorating yoga practice at Ernie Miller Nature Center. This is yoga for anybody. All are welcome, no experience necessary! This class will focus on gentle stretching, serenity, and rejuvenation and will be tailored to individual abilities. Please bring your mat to class. Instructor is Robin Olson-ERYT (Experienced Registered Yoga Teacher). For more information call 913-826-2800. Advanced registration required.
