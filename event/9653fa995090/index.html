Arts Council Concert – Susie Carter

When: Friday, November 13, 2026 at 7:00 PM

Where: West Bountiful Police Department, West Bountiful, UT
550 N 800 W, West Bountiful

The West Bountiful Arts Council concludes its 2026 concert series by featuring Susie Carter’s holiday performance, It’s Beginning to Look a Lot Like Christmas. Concertgoers will be captivated by Susie’s melodic journey from iconic Broadway tunes to beloved holiday classics. Her vocal versatility is sure to touch hearts and herald the festive spirit of the holiday season. Gather your loved ones and join us on Friday, November 13, at 7:00 p.m. at the West Bountiful City Hall, 550 North 800 West, for Susie Carter’s heartwarming holiday concert.
