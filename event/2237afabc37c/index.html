Great Salt Lake State Park, 1498 W 1075 S, Layton, UT, United States

Join a ranger on the back deck of the Visitor Center for a free, informal talk about the rich history, culture, and natural beauty of Great Salt Lake. These short programs offer engaging insights and invite visitors to see the park and the lake with new appreciation. Topics vary and may include information about the local wildlife and plants, exploration, the resorts, or sailing. These talks typically last 15-20 minutes and are suitable for all ages.
