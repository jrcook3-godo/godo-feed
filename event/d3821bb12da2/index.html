Deer Creek Express

When: Now through Saturday, October 31, 2026
Where: Heber Valley Railroad, Heber City, UT

Open in Go Do — see who’s going

Address: 450 S 600 W, Heber City

This scenic 90 minute trip offers incredible views of the Wasatch Back Mountains, the wide-open vistas of Deer Creek Reservoir, and the beautiful Heber Valley. The Deer Creek Express departs from the depot, follows the shores of Deer Creek Reservoir to Decker Bay (about 7 track miles), and returns. Snacks are available for purchase in the depot prior to boarding and on board the train.
