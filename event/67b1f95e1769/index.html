Resolution Adventure Race

When: Saturday, January 2, 2027 at 8:00 AM
Where: Syracuse, UT

Open in Go Do — see who’s going

Address: 4528 West 1700 South, Syracuse, UT, 84075

Races: Resolution Sprint Adventure Race Soloist, Resolution - 2 Person, Resolution - 3 Person, Resolution - 4 Person, Resolution Family Adventure Race - Solo (16 years and older), Volunteers. Get ready for a New Years Resolution Adventure Race that blends trekking, biking, and navigation across the rugged beauty of Utah and Antelope Island State Park. We offer two options: 1) A 5 hour sprint adventure race suitable for beginner to intermediate trail runners, mountain bikers, and will require navigation with a map and compass; 2) Family-friendly 2 hour adventure race. Children 15 and under are free and must be accompanied by an adult; each adult requires a registration.
