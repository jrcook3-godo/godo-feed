CPR Class

When: Wednesday, November 18, 2026 at 6:00 PM

Where: West Jordan Community & Arts Center, West Jordan, UT
8105 South 2200 West, West Jordan

Come take a CPR class with our firefighters at Station 53 in Jordan Landing (7602 Jordan Landing Blvd. West Jordan, UT 84081). Our CPR course trains citizens in CPR, AED, and First Aid use. It is developed from the American Heart Association Heartsaver® course and is a perfect introduction to this life-saving skill. The class provides an AHA certification card and is valid for 2 years. You will leave this class feeling confident in your abilities and ready to face any emergency that comes your way. You can make a difference, get certified today! Register today!
