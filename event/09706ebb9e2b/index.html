November Homeschool Day- Fossils!

When: Wednesday, November 18, 2026 at 11:00 AM

Where: Brigham City Museum of Art and History, Brigham City, UT
24 N 300 W, Brigham City

Price: Free

.kb-image76027_1c0ea5-ff .kb-image-has-overlay:after{opacity:0.3;} @ Join the Brigham City Museum for Homeschool Day! All homeschool, online, daycare, etc. groups are invited to visit the museum on the third Wednesday of every month at 11:00am for a special tour, activity, and scavenger hunt! This month we will focus on Rocks and Fossils! Come and learn about the different kinds of fossils, and have an exclusive look at some of the fossils in our collection. Registration is encouraged.
