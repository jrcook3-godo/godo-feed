Women's Retreat

When: Thursday, October 8, 2026 at 2:00 PM – Saturday, October 10, 2026

Where: Alpine Bible Church, Lehi, UT
310 E State St, Lehi, UT, 84043

Price: $150

Ladies, you are invited to a relaxing getaway at Bear Lake for our annual Women's Retreat October 8th-10th. The cost is $150/person, which covers food, lodging, and other materials. The first session will take place after dinner on Thursday, and the last session will be Saturday morning before cleanup. There are 40 beds available at the house. If you are willing to bring an air mattress to sleep, please let Stacy Wall know in advance. Thanks!The Carpool will leave from ABC at 2:00pm. Directions will be given to each car before departure. If you have any questions, contact Stacy Wall: swall@alp
