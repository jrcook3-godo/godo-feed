I Have Never Really Quite Gotten Over Spence Lecture & Reading

When: Thursday, October 15, 2026 at 6:00 PM

Where: Utah Pride Festival, Salt Lake City, UT
255 E 400 S, Salt Lake City

On 23 October 1966—exactly sixty years ago—Spence Mendenhall was found shot to death in his Salt Lake City home in the Avenues. His murder remains unsolved. Spence was a Gay man who was secretly out to some people but largely lived a closeted life, and his death has since been recognized as a hate crime. Drawing from their own research and personal reflections, Julian Hendrix will explore Spence’s life, death, and legacy.
