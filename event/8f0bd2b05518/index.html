Bus Trip: Red Butte Gardens

When: Monday, October 5, 2026 at 9:30 AM

Where: Park City Senior Center, Park City, UT
1361 Woodside Ave, Park City

Discover the Beauty of Red Butte Garden Join us for a beautiful day of nature, exploration, and fall foliage at Red Butte Garden in Salt Lake City! As one of the largest botanical gardens in the Intermountain West, Red Butte Garden features more than 3,000 unique species of plants and trees across 21 acres of beautifully themed gardens and approximately 75 acres of natural areas. During our visit, we’ll have the opportunity to take in the changing colors of the season as the garden comes alive with vibrant shades of gold, red, orange, and green.
