37th Annual Helper Utah’s Christmas Town Festival

When: Friday, December 4, 2026 at 4:00 PM – Saturday, December 5, 2026

Where: Helper Main Street, Helper, UT
Gazebo Park, Helper

🎄 37th Annual Helper, Utah’s Christmas Town FestivalFriday & Saturday, December 4-5,2027 Join us in the heart of Helper for a weekend full of holiday cheer at the 36th Annual Christmas Town Festival! Enjoy a cozy chili dinner each night from 4:00–7:00 p.m., then bundle up for the beloved Light Parade at 7:00 p.m. — with a spectacular fireworks show immediately following Saturday’s parade! While you’re there, don’t miss the silent raffle, bake sale, and this year’s commemorative Christmas ornaments (limited supply available!).
