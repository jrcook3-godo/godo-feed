Telescope Viewing

When: Friday, October 2, 2026 at 9:00 PM

Where: Meet at the Gifford House, approximately one-mile down the Scenic Drive, Capitol Reef National Park, Capitol Reef, UT

Price: Free

Meet at the Gifford House. Learn about the night sky and view celestial wonders above Capitol Reef. Dress warmly and bring a headlamp or flashlight (a red light if you have one) -- the park is dark at night! Extra parking available across the street and at picnic area. Programs may be cancelled due to weather or emergencies. Check at visitor center for updated daily program offerings.
