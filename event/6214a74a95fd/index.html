City E-Waste and Shredding Event

When: Saturday, November 7, 2026 at 10:00 AM

Where: Canyon View Credit Union Community & Arts Center, West Jordan, UT
8105 2200 W, West Jordan

Every year, the city holds four FREE E-waste and shredding events. Residents can bring up to two ‘Bankers Boxes’ of paper for shredding and residential electronic waste. Documents will be shredded on site in the parking lot of West Jordan Public Works Department (7960 S 4000 W, West Jordan UT 84081) Items need to be drop-off ready. Paper needs to be loose, remove large binder clips, rubber bands, and binders. Bring proof of resident or city employment to participate.
