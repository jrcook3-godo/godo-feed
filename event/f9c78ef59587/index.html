Mysterious X Conference Sunday Pass

When: Sunday, September 27, 2026 at 10:00 AM

Where: Historic Redstone Theatre, Kanab, UT
29 W. Center St., Kanab

Mysterious X 2026 where Mystery, the UnExplained, UFO/UAP, the Paranormal, Alternate History, and Lost Treasure come together. Join researchers, investigators, experiencers, and explorers in Kanab, Utah, a region legendary for UFO/UAP sightings, Ancient Mystery, History, and Lost Treasure. Live talks, panels, Q&A, and film screenings at the Historic RedStone Theater, plus nighttime UFO and UAP CE5 expeditions, investigations at secret locations, and the exclusive Three Lakes Canyon Overnight Expedition tied to UAP and Bigfoot phenomena and the generations long search for Montezumas Aztec treasure. Speakers and Special Guests As Seen On Ancient Aliens, America Unearthed, Ghost Hunters, Beyond Oak Island, Lost Gold of the Aztecs, and Mystery at Blind Frog Ranch. Speakers: Dan Dillman, Scott Wolter, Bertram Tsavadawa, Daryl Marston, Josh Feldman, Ron Feldman, Terry Carter, Dustin Eskelsen, Tom Bennett, Lon Child, Ryan Burns, Levi Harris, Janet Wolter, Robert Kryder.
