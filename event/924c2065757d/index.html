Early Intervention Kids Holiday party

When: Tuesday, December 1, 2026 at 10:00 AM

Where: Kids on the Move – Orem, Orem, UT
475 WEST 260 NORTH, Orem

Price: Free

Early Intervention Kids Holiday party KOTM Early Intervention is hosting a Kids Holiday Party. Come see Santa, sing songs and make some crafts. Open to all current enrolled EI families. The party is one hour long, at 10-11 am and 6-7 pm. It will be held at the KOTM building in Orem, in the multipurpose room. Families are welcome to attend. Email dlawson@kotm.org if you have any questions regarding this event.
