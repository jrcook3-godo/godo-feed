NH: Live Nativity

When: Monday, December 7, 2026
Where: Nature Hills Farm, Cedar City, UT

Open in Go Do — see who’s going

Address: 4326 N 2100 E, Cedar City

Nature Hills: Live Nativity is held from 5:30 pm to 7:30 pm at Nature Hills Farm (4326 N 2100 E, Cedar City). Tickets are available online at naturehillsfarm.com. Nature Hills invites everyone to gather at the Farm as it is transformed into a magical Winter Wonderland with lights and Christmas displays and enjoy a 20 - 30 minute live and interactive tour through the scenes of the Live Nativity.
