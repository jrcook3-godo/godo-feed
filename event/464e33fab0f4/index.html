Guest Speaker Diane Musho Hamilton - Working with the Energy of Anger

When: Thursday, October 29, 2026
Where: Park City, UT

Open in Go Do — see who’s going

Address: 1255 Park Ave, Park City

We are honored to welcome Diane Hamilton for a special evening event! Diane combines decades of experience in conflict resolution with deep practice in meditation. Through her work, Diane invites us to engage difference with curiosity and compassion while discovering a deeper sense of shared humanity. Working with the Energy of Anger Anger is one of our most powerful emotions—and one we often handle poorly. Most of us either suppress our anger or get swept away by it.
