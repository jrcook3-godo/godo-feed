Chamber of Commerce Golf Tournament

When: Friday, October 2, 2026 – Saturday, October 3, 2026

Where: Carbon Country Club & Golf Course, Helper, UT
3055 N Highway 6, Helper

Price: $300

Join the Carbon County Chamber of Commerce for its Golf Tournament at the Carbon Country Club, October 2–3. All proceeds from the tournament will benefit the Nurses Banquet. Golfers can choose scheduled tee times beginning at noon on Friday, October 2, or participate in the shotgun start at 9:00 a.m. on Saturday, October 3. Lunch is included on either day, and the winning team and drawing winners will be announced Saturday. Registration is $300 per team for Chamber members and $400 per team for non-members.
