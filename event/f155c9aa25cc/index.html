Writing Workshop: Getting Unstuck and Making Inspiration

When: Wednesday, October 21, 2026 at 7:00 PM
Where: Grand County Library, Moab, UT
Price: Free

Open in Go Do — see who’s going

Address: 257 E Center St, Moab

Suzanne Roberts, author of "Bad Tourist," "Almost Somewhere," "Animal Bodies," and "52 Writing Prompts: Inspiration for the Creative Writer" is back to help you hone your craft. Writer’s block comes from the fear that we don’t have anything interesting or useful, entertaining or important to say. Or sometimes, a story is burrowed inside us, but we just don’t know where to begin. Join us in a fun, hands-on creative writing workshop, where we’ll work to get unstuck, move through our blocks, and make—rather than find—inspiration.
