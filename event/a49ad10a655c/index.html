From Farm to Distribution: Becoming a Charlie’s Produce Supplier

When: Thursday, October 1, 2026 at 2:00 PM

Where: Utah Department of Agriculture and Food, Taylorsville, UT
4315 S 2700 W 2nd Floor, Suite 2200, Taylorsville

Learn how Utah producers can work with Charlie’s Produce to supply local food to schools and other institutions. This 75-minute webinar will walk through the vendor application, producer requirements, pricing and packaging expectations, food safety and GAP certification, and next steps for getting your products into the distribution system. A live Q&A will provide an opportunity to get answers directly from the Charlie’s Produce team. Registration link
