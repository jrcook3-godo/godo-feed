Concealed Weapons Permit Class

When: Saturday, November 21, 2026 at 10:00 AM

Where: North Springs Range, Price, UT
5245 S 8000 W, Price

Price: $60

Looking to get your Utah Concealed Carry Permit? Join us for a comprehensive class that includes everything you need to get started! The class covers: Passport photos Fingerprints Application Range day pass 2025 Fee: $60 per person Class Start Time: 10:00 AM Class Duration: Approximately 4 hours Pro Tip: Bring snacks and beverages to keep energized throughout the class! Payment Options: Cash, check, or Venmo (credit cards are not accepted). Firearms: While firearms are not required for the class, feel free to bring your gear to enjoy the range afterwards.
