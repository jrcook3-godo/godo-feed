Hometown Holiday Market

When: Saturday, November 28, 2026
Where: Parowan Main Street, Parowan, UT

Open in Go Do — see who’s going

Parowan Hometown Holiday Market is held from 10:00 am - 4:00 pm in Parowan shopping areas throughout the community filled with local artisans, small businesses & amazing food. Shop small, shop local, and shop with Parowan this holiday season for unique & meaningful gifts for your loved ones.
