Craftober Fest Plein Air Silent Auction

When: Thursday, October 15, 2026 at 6:00 PM
Where: Level Crossing Brewery, South Salt Lake, UT

Open in Go Do — see who’s going

Address: 2496 S W Temple St, South Salt Lake, UT 84115, USA

Join us at Level Crossing Brewery for a celebration of local talent at our Craftober Fest Silent Auction! Hosted by SSL Arts, this special event showcases stunning plein air artwork created live by eight incredible local artists in the brewery. It's your chance to wander the exhibition, meet the artists in person, and bid on your favorite one-of-a-kind pieces. Best of all, 100% of the auction proceeds go directly to the individual artists, making the perfect opportunity to take home beautiful new art while directly supporting our creative community. Special note, you must be 21+ to enter Lev
