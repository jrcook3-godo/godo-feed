Food Distribution

When: Saturday, January 2, 2027 at 8:30 AM

Where: Good Shepherd, Sandy, UT
8575 South 700 East, Sandy

Price: Free

Good Shepherd has partnered with the Utah Food Bank and is hosting their Mobile Food Pantry program. Details on the Utah Food Bank’s Mobile Pantry program can be found on their website (utahfoodbank.org). On Saturday morning the event will be hosted in the Good Shepherd parking lot and food will be provided to the first 180 families. Food is provided in a drive thru format free of charge. The event will be held from 8:30 to 9:30 AM or until the food is all distributed. SIGN UP TO VOLUNTEER HERE
