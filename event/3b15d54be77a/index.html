First Aid/CPR Certification Class

When: Thursday, October 1, 2026 at 5:00 PM

Where: - Kaysville Fire Station 61 175 South Main Street Kaysville UT 84037, Kaysville, UT
- Kaysville Fire Station 61 175 South Main Street Kaysville UT 84037

Learn CPR & Save a Life! The Kaysville City Fire Department is offering CPR Certification Classes at the Kaysville Fire Station. Classes require a minimum of 3 students and have a limit of 12. Participants will receive an official Red Cross CPR Certification Card upon completion, valid for 2 years. Registration fees are due at the time of registration and are non-refundable. Whether you’re a parent, teacher, coach, or just want to be prepared—this training could help you save a life. https://www.kaysville.gov/calendar.aspx?EID=2786
