Paul Sheffield at Historic Ogden

When: Friday, December 4, 2026 – Saturday, December 5, 2026
Where: Historic Ogden, Ogden, UT
Price: $20

Open in Go Do — see who’s going

Address: 269 25th St, Ogden, Utah 84401

Paul is a Wiseguys' favorite that has been doing stand up comedy now for 15 years. He just released his 5th full length comedy special 'In My Mind' in February of this year, and is excited to add this all new special to his album catalog. He is also the host of the new Youtube show 'Best Thing On The Menu'. He tastes entire menus while giving a comedic take on what to eat and what to avoid at every restaurant he visits. His other albums: “Sheffield Had it Worse”(2009), “Married to the Mic”(2010), “A Small H.R. Issue”(2014), and “Sanity at its Finest”(2018) are available and streaming
