Arches in the Evening: Art Tailgate Party

When: Saturday, October 3, 2026 at 5:00 PM

Where: Park Avenue Trailhead Parking Lot, Arches National Park, Moab, UT

Price: Free

Attend a special event hosted by the 2026 Community Artist in the Parks Chrissy Noel Kinslow. Pull up, paint, play and watch the light disappear! Attendees can bring their own supplies or borrow from the artist. Please bring a camp chair, snacks, curiosity, and creativity!
