Domínguez-Escalante 250

When: Thursday, November 19, 2026
Where: BYU Music Building, Provo, UT

Open in Go Do — see who’s going

Address: 1100 900 E, Provo

BYU composer Steven Ricks collaborates with BYU English faculty member Stephen Tuttle and BYU graphic design faculty member Brent Barson to create a multimedia concert piece inspired by the journals and explorations of Franciscan friars Francisco Atanasio Domínguez and Silvestre Vélez de Escalante. Supported by a 2026 Interdisciplinary Studies Grant from the Charles Redd Center for Western Studies, this piece will be performed by the St. George-based ensemble Grand Circle New Music.November 19, 7:30 p.m.THE BOX (MB)Free
