Little Haunts

When: Friday, October 9, 2026 at 4:00 AM
Where: Emigration Canyon, UT

Fall is the time for Little Haunts, a family friendly activity during the day. This "not so scary fun is for young and old". Spooks can come in costume or just how they are and trick-or-treat throughout the Village. Guests enjoy the costume parade, and children love to listen to the Story Telling Witch sharing spooky tales! Little Haunts is all fun and no frights!
