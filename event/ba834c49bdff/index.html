Guided Walk

When: Saturday, October 10, 2026 at 4:00 PM

Where: Meet in front of the Gifford House. Parking available across the street in the dirt lot or at the picnic area., Capitol Reef National Park, Capitol Reef, UT

Price: Free

Meet outside the Gifford House for an easy to moderate one-hour walk with a ranger. Bring water; wear good shoes and sun protection. Parking available across the street in the dirt lot. Programs may be cancelled due to weather or emergencies. Check at the Visitor Center for updated program listings.
