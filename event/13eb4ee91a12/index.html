5% Day for the Moab Valley Multicultural Center

When: Tuesday, October 13, 2026 at 8:00 AM
Where: Moonflower Community Cooperative, 39 E 100 N, Moab, UT, 84532, United States, Moab, UT

Open in Go Do — see who’s going

Address: 39 E 100 N, Moab

Join us all day Tuesday, October 13th in supporting the Moab Valley Multicultural Center for this month’s 5% Day! The Moab Valley Multicultural Center’s mission is: To build a safe, healthy, and culturally vibrant community for all. Construir una comunidad segura, saludable y culturalmente vibrante para todos. MVMC serves the community in four program areas: Crisis Resource and Advocacy Interpretation and Translation Language and Life Skills Community Engagement and Outreach MVMC is a walk-in center that welcomes anyone and everyone. Please join us at the MVMC to celebrate Día de los Muerto
