Victory Marathon medal Virtual Race Challenge Coin Challenge

When: Now through Friday, February 26, 2027

Where: West Jordan, UT
8831 S Redwood Rd, West Jordan, UT, 84088

Races: Victory marathon medal Virtual Race Challenge Coin Challenge. In the battle of Marathon (490 BC) an army of Athenians faced off against an army of Persians over twice their size. Through brilliant battle strategy and the valor of each Grecian soldier defending their homeland, the Persian army was driven back and defeated. The messenger and solder, Pheidippides, ran to deliver the victorious message of the battle to Athens over 25 miles away from Marathon. You too can run to deliver a message of victory and receive this victory medal! Activity type: Running or Alternate activity Challenge requirements: • Wear your most patriotic gear when completing this
