October #LoveYourWatershed Cleanup

When: Saturday, October 10, 2026 at 10:00 AM

Where: Triumph Trailhead, West Jordan, UT
West Jordan

Price: Free

Trash and debris from roadway curbs and gutters enters the Jordan River where it is difficult to remove. Make a habit of volunteering on the second Saturday of the month with your friends, family, and community and help us prevent garbage from getting into the Jordan River. We provide tools, water, & snacks. Dress for the weather, come ready to have fun and make a difference. Location: 7000 S, West Jordan. Park at Triumph Trailhead https://maps.app.goo.gl/cPAZsUkDT4N7q73w5 Registration required. Location subject to change.
