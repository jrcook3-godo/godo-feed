I BELIEVE IN YOU x RUN Cedar Valley - Half Marathon, 10k, 5k, Kids

When: Saturday, April 10, 2027 at 6:30 AM

Where: Eagle Mountain, UT
1300 N Harmony Way, Eagle Mountain, UT, 84005

Races: Half Marathon, 10K Run, 5k Run, Kids Run. We changed the date! Join us in April for the FIRST EVER "I BELIEVE IN YOU" x RUN Cedar Valley Half Marathon, a 13.1 mile road-meets-dirt race starting at Cedar Fort Rodeo Grounds, winding along Cory B. Wride Memorial Highway and ending at Sage Canyon Middle School in Eagle Mountain, UT. 10k, 5k, and Kids run will also be a part of the festivities. We invite you to show your support by running with your own “I BELIEVE IN YOU” sign and run with the man who started it all, Runner Guy WIth A Sign, through the Eagle Mountain and Cedar Fort communities, experience an epic sunrise over Mount Timpano
