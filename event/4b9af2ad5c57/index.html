BYU Dancesport Championships

When: Friday, November 13, 2026 at 8:00 AM – Saturday, November 14, 2026

Where: Wilkinson Student Center, Provo, UT
1060 E Campus Dr,, Provo

Price: $9

The BYU Dancesport Championships brings together students from all ballroom classes to compete and celebrate their progress. The event also includes categories open to the general public in Latin, Standard, Smooth, and Rhythm, creating an exciting and inclusive showcase of talent, energy, and passio
