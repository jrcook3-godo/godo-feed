Stride for Safety: A Virtual Race for Friends Against Family Violence

When: Now through Saturday, October 31, 2026

Where: Vernal, UT
224 East 500 South, Vernal, UT, 84078

Races: 5.5k Virtual Event. Stride for Safety: A Virtual Race for Friends Against Family ViolenceEvery mile. Every step. Every survivor matters. Join us for Stride for Safety, a virtual 5.5K benefiting Friends Against Family Violence (FAFV). Whether you run, walk, jog, or move at your own pace, you can turn your miles into meaningful support for individuals and families impacted by family violence. FAFV’s mission is to provide services to those impacted by family and sexual violence, increase offender accountability and raise awareness of the impact of interpersonal violence on our families and community.
