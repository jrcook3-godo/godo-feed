First Friday Holiday Concert

When: Friday, December 4, 2026 at 6:00 PM

Where: West Jordan Community & Arts Center, West Jordan, UT
8105 South 2200 West, West Jordan

Join us in Partnership with the Salt Lake County Library and Excellence in the Community for a festive evening in West Jordan. Experience the sparkle of the holidays as music fills the Viridian Event Center and don’t miss a special mid-evening visit from Santa Claus, bringing cheer to children and grown-ups alike! The celebration begins at 6:00 PM at the Viridian Event Center (8030 S 1825 W) with s’mores and hot cocoa in the outdoor amphitheater. (Supplies are limited so don’t miss out!)
