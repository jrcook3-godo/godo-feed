Night Sky Program

When: Friday, October 2, 2026 at 8:00 PM

Where: Meet at the Campfire Circle near Site 22 in Loop B of the Green River Campground on the Jensen, UT side of the park., Dinosaur National Monument, Green River, UT

Price: Free

Planned Activity: Night Sky for the Naked Eye Join park rangers for a short talk followed by stargazing or moonviewing (weather permitting). Meet at the Campfire Circle near Site 22 in the Green River Campground. Keep flashlights pointed at the ground, red covers available. Parking for non-campers is available in the day use area. Please Note : Program topics can change due to unforeseen schedule changes. Programs may also be canceled due to adverse weather, for visitor or employee safety, or because of staffing shortages caused by emergencies such as fire or medical conditions.
