Sunday Celebration In Person

When: Sunday, March 28, 2027 at 11:00 AM

Where: Masonic Lodge, 262 S. Mall Dr., Santa Clara, UT
Masonic Lodge, 262 S. Mall Dr.

Join us in person for our Sunday Celebration at 262 S Mall Dr, St. George (lower level of the Masonic Lodge). Doors open at 10:30 AM for connection and community. Our service begins at 11:00 AM and includes uplifting music, an inspiring teaching, affirmative prayer, and time to connect with your spiritual family. Come as you are. All are welcome.
