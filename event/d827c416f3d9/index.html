Evening Programs at Wahweap Amphitheater

When: Saturday, October 3, 2026 at 7:00 PM

Where: The Wahweap amphitheater is located in the Wahweap Campground, across from the campground store. From the intersection of Lakeshore Dr and Wahweap Dr, go straight for about 1000 ft, then turn left at the "Wahweap Campground" sign. This parking lot also hosts the RV dump station., Glen Canyon National Recreation Area, Big Water, UT

Sit back and enjoy a presentation from one of Glen Canyon’s Park Rangers. Topics Vary. In the event of cloud cover, rain, lightning, or other poor weather conditions, this event will be cancelled.
