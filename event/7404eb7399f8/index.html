Preschool Program: Fantastic Fish

When: Wednesday, April 28, 2027 at 10:00 AM

Where: Bear River Migratory Bird Refuge - 2155 W Forest Street Brigham City UT 84302, Brigham City, UT
Bear River Migratory Bird Refuge - 2155 W Forest Street Brigham City UT 84302

Dive into the fascinating world of fish! Preschoolers will discover what makes a fish a fish and learn about the adaptations that help them survive underwater. Activities include a fish hide-and-seek game and a colorful fish lantern craft. https://www.boxeldercountyut.gov/calendar.aspx?EID=1259
