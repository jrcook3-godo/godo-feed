To reach the Nature Center by car, drive about 1/2 mile northeast from the park's South Entrance. There will be a sign for the Nature Center. Turn there and park in the designated parking lots. Limited spaces are available. If parking is full, the Nature Center is only a 10-15 minute walk from the Zion Canyon Visitor Center.
