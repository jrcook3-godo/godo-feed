Chair Yoga @ the Grand Center

10 upcoming dates in Moab, Utah. Next: Tuesday, October 13, 2026 at 2:30 PM.

Address: 182 N 500 W, Moab

Join us every Tuesday afternoon at 2:30 PM at the Grand Center for a gentle, accessible Chair Yoga class led by rotating Wellness Collective yoga instructors. Each teacher brings their own style and expertise, while sharing a common focus on safety, choice, and ease in the body. This class is especially supportive for older adults and anyone who prefers to stay off the floor, has limited mobility, uses a chair for stability, or is simply looking for a calm, nourishing way to move.
