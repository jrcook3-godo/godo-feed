Music & Movement Storytime Class – Lehi at Lehi Music & Movement

5 upcoming dates in Lehi, Utah · Free. Next: Friday, October 23, 2026 at 10:30 AM.

Address: 265 N 400 W, Lehi

Music & Movement Storytime is a great class for your toddler. If you’re interested in your child building literacy and communication skills through songs, finger plays, group book reading and movement activities – you need to attend these classes! This is a free class. Friday classes at the Lehi Public Library from 10:30-11:15am starting September 11 – November 20, 2026. (*No class on Oct 16) Email Jessica Nix at jnix@kotm.org to sign up or learn more.
