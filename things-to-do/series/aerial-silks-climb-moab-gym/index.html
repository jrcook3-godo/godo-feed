Aerial Silks at Climb Moab Gym

24 upcoming dates in Moab, Utah · $35. Next: Sunday, October 11, 2026 at 7:30 PM.

Address: 11850 S Highway 191 Ste A8, Moab

Tuesdays from 8am-9am & Sundays from 7:30-8:30pm Join Chelsea Manning for this series on aerial silks! Join as a drop in, or become a regular and level up your silks skills. Learn the techniques of this beautiful movement practice with the support of an experienced teacher. Classes run for 60 minutes and are limited to 8 participants. Bring stretchy comfortable clothes that you feel good moving around in! Age Limit 16 and up Chelsea Manning came to Moab in 2023 to pursue skydiving after a lifetime of visiting Moab for rock climbing and canyoneering. Chelsea has a background in music pedagogy
