Garth Brooks – Blame It All On My Roots Arena Tour at Delta Center

4 upcoming dates in Salt Lake City, Utah. Next: Thursday, October 8, 2026.

Address: 301 S Temple, Salt Lake City, UT 84101

Garth Brooks is bringing the Blame It All On My Roots Arena Tour to the Delta Center on October 8, 9, 10, and 11! Tickets go on sale Wednesday, September 23 at 10AM MT. The waiting room opens 30 minutes before the on sale begins. Bags are not allowed inside. Diaper and medical bags will be allowed if they are no larger than 14”x14”x6”. Clutches will be allowed but must be no larger than 4”x6”x2”. Be advised that the only authorized ticketing outlets for events at Delta Center are DeltaCenter.com, UtahJazz.com, UtahMammoth.com, and SeatGeek.com.
