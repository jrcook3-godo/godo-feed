Address: University Ave 550 N, 84601 Provo, Utah County, United States

In reading lab we: practice and expand our reading skills with a variety of texts such as news articles, poems, fiction, and non-fiction essays learn vocabulary and practice pronunciation. explore the context of what we read by doing research and discussing what we already know about a topic. learn about authors and writing summaries of what we read as well as connecting material to our own opinions helps with comprehension. practice reading (usually out loud) many different genres of writing and asking questions about the material. It’s a lot of fun and always interesting! 550 N. University
