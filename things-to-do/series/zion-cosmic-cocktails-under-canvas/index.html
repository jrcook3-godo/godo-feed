Zion: Cosmic Cocktails at Under Canvas

5 upcoming dates in Bryce Canyon City, Utah. Next: Saturday, October 10, 2026 at 5:00 PM.

Address: 1325 S John's Valley Road, Bryce Canyon City

Sip on creative cocktails and mocktails inspired by the stars. Select from a variety of specialty drinks—such as Space Odyssey, Meteor Mimosas, and Shower or Stars—that incorporate edible glitter, pop rocks, and celestial-themed garnishes. Or choose non-alcoholic beverages such as Moon Milk, Starburst Cider, and sleep-focused herbal tea blends. These refreshments are a perfect way to toast the galaxies on a beautiful, star-filled night.
