Special Education 101 at Zoom

10 upcoming dates in Murray, Utah. Next: Tuesday, October 27, 2026 at 7:00 PM.

Address: Zoom

You are your child’s most important advocate. This training will give you the knowledge and confidence to make sure they receive the support they need. You will learn how special education works, understand your rights, and discover practical strategies to become an equal partner with your child’s school. Whether your child is just starting the evaluation process or already has an IEP, you’ll leave with tools you can use right away to help your child succeed.
