Bird Walk at Fielding Garr Ranch

52 upcoming dates in Syracuse, Utah. Next: Wednesday, May 26, 2027 at 9:00 AM.

Meet at the picnic area at Fielding Garr Ranch. We’ll walk through the property’s densely populated wetland in search of your bucket-list bird. Binoculars are available to borrow for this event. There is no additional charge for this activity – regular park entry fees apply. Contact dpittz@utah.gov with any questions.
