Carbon & Emery Farmers Market at Sutherlands

4 upcoming dates in Price, Utah. Next: Saturday, October 10, 2026 at 9:00 AM.

Address: 406 State Hwy 55, Price

Shop local every Saturday at the Carbon Emery Farmers Market! Browse fresh produce, homemade baked goods, handcrafted items, local products, and more while supporting farmers, artisans, and small businesses from Carbon and Emery counties. The market is open every Saturday from July 18 through October, beginning at 9:00 a.m., and is located in Sutherland’s parking lot in Price. Come early for the best selection, meet your local vendors, and enjoy a fun community shopping experience.
