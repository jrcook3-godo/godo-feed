Advocate Connect at Utah Office for Victims of Crime

4 upcoming dates in Salt Lake City, Utah. Next: Monday, October 12, 2026 at 2:00 PM.

Address: 420 E S Temple St #500, Salt Lake City

Register for Advocate Connect HERE. Timpanogos Legal Center, Utah Crime Victims Legal Clinic, and Utah Office for Victims of Crime are hosting a monthly virtual open forum; where advocates can bring common challenges and specific cases and find trauma-informed solutions and ideas. Advocates can bring their questions and their expertise to help other advocates throughout the state.
