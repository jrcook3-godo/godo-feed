Utah Veteran Entrepreneur Networking Event at The Mill Entrepreneurship Center, 9690 S 300 W, Sandy, UT 84070, USA

6 upcoming dates in Sandy, Utah · Free. Next: Friday, October 16, 2026 at 11:30 AM.

Address: The Mill Entrepreneurship Center, 9690 S 300 W, Sandy, UT 84070, USA

For veterans, active duty, reserve, guard, and military spouses, come showcase your business and network with other veteran-owned businesses. You can reserve one of 10 spots to speak at the networking event or you can come as an attendee. Free to attend.Lunch is provided generously by our sponsor, American United Federal Credit Union. Come and network with other veterans and get to know the Utah Veteran Business Resource Center and the resources available to you through their office. Register here: https://www.tickettailor.com/events/utahveteranbusinessresourcecenter/1204189
