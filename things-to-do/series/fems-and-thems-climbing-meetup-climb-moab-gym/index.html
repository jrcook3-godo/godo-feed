Fems & Thems Climbing Meetup at Climb Moab Gym

12 upcoming dates in Moab, Utah · $10. Next: Thursday, October 15, 2026 at 6:00 PM.

Address: 11850 S Highway 191 Ste A8, Moab

Our monthly Fems & Thems meetup is every Thursday! Our Fems and Thems community meetup is meant to foster an inclusive space for folks who might otherwise feel intimidated by stepping into a male-dominated sport. Come hang out, get to know the existing community, and learn from people who love climbing! All experiences welcome, shoe rentals included. $10 entry fee, FREE for existing members
