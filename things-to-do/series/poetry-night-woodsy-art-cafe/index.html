Poetry Night at Woodsy Art Cafe

5 upcoming dates in Logan, Utah. Next: Tuesday, October 20, 2026 at 7:00 PM.

Address: 53 E 100 N, Logan

Step into the rhythm of words and the magic of verse at our cozy Poetry Night! Whether you're a passionate poet or simply love the sound of a well-crafted line, this is your space to share, listen, and be inspired. Bring a favorite poem—your own or a beloved classic—and join a welcoming circle of fellow word lovers. No pressure, just poetry, good vibes, and the joy of expression. Let your voice be heard or simply soak in the beauty of language. Everyone’s welcome—come find your muse!
