Eucharist at Chapel

13 upcoming dates in Salt Lake City, Utah. Next: Sunday, November 1, 2026 at 9:30 AM.

Address: Chapel

The Holy Eucharist is the principal act of Christian life, the holy meal that sustains every follower of Jesus in their vocation. Our 9:30 a.m. Eucharist is a quiet, spoken service held in the courtyard during the summer months and the chapel during colder parts of the year. All are welcome.
