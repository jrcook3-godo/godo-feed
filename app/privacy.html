Go Do Privacy Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026 · Test version

Go Do helps you find things to do around Utah's Wasatch Front and make plans with friends. This page explains what the app stores about you, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we store

  • Your account: email address, password (stored scrambled; we can't read it), name, username, city, optional bio and profile photo.
  • What you do in the app: events and activities you mark Go Do, Maybe or No Go, things you follow, hide or save, your interests and settings.
  • Social features: friend requests, groups, chat messages, posts, photos, comments, likes, pings, reviews, events you create, and reports you file.
  • Your location is used on your phone to sort things by distance. It is not saved to your account.
  • Your birthday is only checked when you sign up (you must be 13 or older) and is not saved.

Who can see it

  • Other members can see your name, username, city and photo.
  • Friends can see your plans (you can turn this off in Settings › Privacy), your friends-only posts, and events you post for friends.
  • Everyone sees only totals like "12 going," your public posts, your public events and your reviews (first name and last initial).
  • Chats are visible only to the people in them.
  • Your email address is never shown to other members.
  • People you block can't find you, message you, ping you or add you to groups.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information or use it for advertising. Go Do runs on these services, which store data for us:

  • Supabase: accounts, the database and photos.
  • GitHub: hosting for the app and the public events list.
  • Google (Gmail): sending account emails, like password-reset codes.

When you tap links to ticket sellers, ride apps (Uber, Lyft), booking sites (Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com, Expedia) or maps, those companies' own privacy policies apply.

Where listings come from

Events come from public calendars and ticketing sites. Places come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors), and some photos come from Wikimedia Commons and Unsplash, credited in the app.

Links to tickets, rides and places to stay

When you tap a link to buy tickets, book a ride or a room, or make a reservation, it opens inside Go Do and we note which partner you went to (not what you buy). Go Do may earn a small commission from some partners; it never changes the price you pay.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings › Delete My Account. This immediately deletes your profile, plans, friends, groups you own, messages, posts, photos and reviews.

Kids

Go Do is for people 13 and older.

Questions or problems

Use Settings › Report a problem in the app. We'll update this page as Go Do grows and before it goes into the app stores.
