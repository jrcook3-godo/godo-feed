How Go Do reads public event calendars

Go Do lists everything to do in Utah in one free app, with a link back to the organizer for tickets and details. To keep it complete, a small program called GoDoBot reads public event calendars — venues, cities, libraries, parks, schools and organizers — a few times a day.

How it behaves

  • It identifies itself honestly as GoDoBot with a link to this page.
  • It follows robots.txt. To keep GoDoBot out of all or part of your site, add a rule for User-agent: GoDoBot (or *).
  • It only reads public event listings, a few pages at a time, and no more than every few hours.
  • Each event on Go Do links back to your page, so people buy tickets and get details from you.

Want your events on Go Do — or corrected, or removed?

The easiest way is to send them to us directly with a free Go Do data feed, so they're always accurate. For anything else, use Contact Us and we'll take care of it quickly.
